# Iris mascot

[`iris-logo.png`](iris-logo.png) is the original Iris mascot used in the repository header.
Its monochrome manga portrait, violet iris flower and circular silhouette connect the identity
to the Hermes ecosystem while giving Iris her own character. The outer background is transparent.

Generated on 30 September 2026 with the built-in `image_gen` tool, using
`transparent_background: true`. The PNG is 1254 × 1254; keep its alpha channel when reusing it.

## Final generation prompt

```text
Create one finished original logo mascot for IRIS, an AI business scout, as a square avatar badge. Visual direction: bold black-and-white manga ink, clean Japanese anime portrait, high contrast and elegant negative space, the same general visual family as a monochrome anime assistant mascot. An adult woman with a confident, observant, warm expression, three-quarter head and shoulders looking slightly to the right while her eyes look toward the viewer. Distinctive side-swept wavy shoulder-length black hair, one swept strand, no blunt bob bangs, a small recognizable iris-flower hair ornament with a restrained vivid violet accent. Original character design, not a copy of the Hermes mascot. Put the portrait within a solid black circular medallion with a crisp white outer ring so it works on both light and dark GitHub backgrounds. White face and shoulders, strong black ink shapes, minimal graceful linework, one violet ornament only. Keep the silhouette exceptionally readable at 64px, big face and eye shapes, avoid intricate hatching. Center the badge with modest transparent space around it. Actual transparent background OUTSIDE the circular badge; inside the badge opaque black and white illustration. Flat graphic artwork, no gradients, no shadows, no realistic rendering. No text, no letters, no watermark, no mockup, no multiple options or contact sheet. Produce a polished single brand asset suitable for a repository header and Telegram avatar.
```

The asset is covered by the repository's [MIT license](../LICENSE).
