# Iris — your AI growth scout

**Know when to react. Know when to hold your price.**

Iris watches the competitors you choose, connects their moves to your Shopify products,
and brings one useful next step to Telegram — in English or Egyptian Arabic.

**[See Iris in action →](docs/DEMO.md)** · **[Connect your store →](docs/SETUP.md)**

## One offer can change the decision

Your sunscreen is EGP 320. Infinity's is EGP 360. Should you cut your price?

Iris checked the product page and found an advertised **buy 2, get 2 free** offer:

| Customer's basket | Your demo listing | Infinity's advertised deal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One bottle | EGP 320 | EGP 360 |
| Four bottles | EGP 1,280 | EGP 720, if eligible |

Your single-bottle price is lower; their four-bottle deal costs less per bottle.
Iris checks the product match, offer conditions and your margin before recommending a cut.

*Demo: 30 September 2026. Synthetic Mira Nile catalog; official competitor prices.
Checkout eligibility and a complete product match remain unverified. [Evidence](docs/DEMO.md).*

## Put her to work

| Task | What Iris does |
| --- | --- |
| **Watch** | Catch relevant price, sale-price and stock changes |
| **Decide** | Compare products and offers; calculate from your cost assumptions |
| **Improve** | Find factual gaps in product photos, descriptions and prices |
| **Draft** | Prepare captions, customer replies and product copy from your store's facts |
| **Follow up** | Remember chosen actions and reported results for the weekly review |

Try: **“Compare my sunscreen with Infinity.”** Then: **“Draft a caption in Egyptian Arabic.”**

## Run Iris for your store

You'll need [Hermes](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent), a Shopify catalog connection,
a Telegram bot and a model provider. **[Follow the setup guide →](docs/SETUP.md)**

Four tools. Three scripts. Hermes handles Telegram, memory and scheduling.
Stores stay read-only; you choose and apply the action.

**Early pilot:** 61 offline tests and a 15-case business evaluator.
Real merchant revenue impact and time savings still need validation.
