# Finding competitor stores

Good candidates:
- Sell the same product types as the owner, to the same customers (same country, similar price range).
- Have a readable online store: `read_store` returns products.
- Are active: recent products, prices in the same currency.

Skip:
- Articles, blogs, comparison sites and review pages.
- Huge general marketplaces as a whole. A specific seller or category page on Jumia or Noon is fine.
- Stores that only exist on Instagram or Facebook. Suggest the owner sends screenshots of those instead.

Explain each suggestion in one line the owner understands, for example:
"Coffee House: sells beans and drip packs at 300–650 EGP, close to your range."
