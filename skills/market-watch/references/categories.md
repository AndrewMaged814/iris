# Category comparison keys

Before comparing products, read the section matching the saved Market profile. Use confirmed
catalog attributes and variant options on both sides; a similar title alone is not equivalence.
Keep marketplace prices separate from official-store prices. Blocked or social-only sellers
need owner screenshots; search snippets do not prove a price or offer.

## Beauty and personal care

Check pack size, form, skin type and advertised offers. A matching size and category is enough
to compare price; don't list every unconfirmed attribute. Compare price per ml when both sizes
are confirmed. Keep bundles and conditional offers separate from a single item's base price.
Equal concentration, formulation or efficacy needs evidence on both sides.
Competitors: brand stores, beauty retailers, pharmacies and marketplaces; social sellers need screenshots.
Example: two confirmed 50 ml sunscreens at EGP 320 and EGP 360 differ by EGP 40 per bottle;
an advertised multi-buy needs its own basket comparison.

## Fashion and shoes

Compare item type, material, size range and relevant construction; exact branded matches also
need model and authenticity evidence. Check availability for the requested size and colour,
not just whether any variant is available. Price per piece or confirmed pack count.
Competitors: brand sites, boutiques, marketplaces and social sellers.
Example: two cotton T-shirts can support a price comparison; a polyester shirt is a different alternative.

## Electronics and accessories

Compare exact model, capacity/storage, condition, included accessories and warranty/region.
For accessories, confirm compatibility and specification (connector, wattage, length).
Price per item or identical kit; a different capacity is a different variant.
Competitors: official dealers, specialist retailers and marketplaces; confirm seller and warranty.
Example: a 128 GB phone at EGP 12,000 does not establish that a 256 GB phone at EGP 14,000 is overpriced.

## Food and drink

Compare product/variety, grade or ingredients, weight/volume, pack count and relevant freshness
or storage conditions. Price per kg, litre or piece when quantities are confirmed; convert
g to kg and ml to litres before comparing. Separate delivery and subscription conditions.
Competitors: producers, grocers, specialty shops, marketplaces and social sellers.
Example: EGP 150 for 250 g is EGP 600/kg; EGP 260 for 500 g is EGP 520/kg.

## Home and furniture

Compare item function, dimensions, material, finish, set count and assembly/delivery inclusions.
For consumables such as candles, confirm weight and any claimed burn time on both sides.
Price per piece/set, metre or confirmed weight; avoid treating a smaller item as an equivalent.
Competitors: furniture/home retailers, workshops, marketplaces and social sellers.
Example: a two-chair set at EGP 4,000 is EGP 2,000 per chair only when the chairs match the single listing.

## Other

Derive comparison keys from the owner's catalog, variant options and owner-confirmed use case.
Confirm them in the Market profile. State the keys used and the missing attribute that could
reverse the decision. Use a confirmed unit of measure, otherwise price per item with size differences.
Find official sellers in the profile's market; ask for screenshots where public evidence is unavailable.
