# Product

## Promise

**Iris is your AI market watcher.**

She closes the gap between running your store and understanding the market around it.
Iris brings competitor activity, public market research and your store’s context together,
helping you see what deserves attention and choose your next move.

**Know what’s changing. See what matters. Make your next move.**

## The owner’s experience

An owner can ask for a market briefing across competitors, drill into one business,
compare relevant products, investigate possible explanations and ask what to prioritize.
Iris connects the findings to the owner’s catalog, stock, costs and goals, then helps with
a grounded draft, a chosen action and a later review.

The product is defined by this research-and-decision relationship. Calculations, product
comparison and offer checks support the work; a single arithmetic or discount case does
not define Iris’s identity.

## The workflow

1. **Observe.** Follow the selected rival product pages and keep dated history.
   Research additional competitors and public sources when the owner asks.
2. **Connect.** Bring related findings across products and businesses into one picture,
   joining them to the confirmed connected catalog and owner context.
3. **Understand.** Explain what matters and investigate relevant context. Distinguish
   observed facts, a competitor’s stated explanation and supported hypotheses.
4. **Recommend.** Prioritize a practical response that fits the owner’s goal and resources.
   Prepare grounded English or Egyptian Arabic copy when requested.
5. **Review.** Remember what the owner chooses. Review reported results against an appropriate
   baseline and use them in the next decision without inventing causal sales lift.

## Current boundaries

One Iris serves one store owner; normal installations restrict access to that owner.
The hosted judge demo explicitly allows visitors to explore the connected Mira Nile demo
shop. Each visitor has their own conversation; app changes, connections and shared memory
remain reserved for the authenticated operator.
Automatic watches cover selected product pages; wider research is on demand.
History has an explicit observation window, and current pages do not establish past changes.
Competitor sales, full market coverage and hidden motives remain unknown without evidence.

Tools return data. Iris writes the advice. Connected app reads and owner-requested writes
use native Composio; requested changes require exact terms and readback before success.
Current live writes remain unverified; scheduled market checks read apps only.

Hermes supplies Telegram, sessions, research, vision, memory, scheduling and delivery.
Iris supplies the market workflow, selected-product history, calculations and interpretation.
[Current verification](STATUS.md) separates supported behavior from trial results and gaps.

## Product direction

Improve the owner’s market picture through stronger cross-competitor product grouping,
richer recorded campaign/catalog/policy context, more reliable investigations and measured
owner outcomes. This is the roadmap, not a claim of delivered automatic coverage.
